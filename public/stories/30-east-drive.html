30 East Drive: The Black Monk of Pontefract

10 min read SSSSS Coordinates: 53.7997 N, 1.5492 W
🏠

Most of the world's famous haunted places are grand: castles, prisons, asylums, great houses with centuries of history. 30 East Drive is none of those. It is an ordinary semi-detached house on an ordinary residential street in Pontefract, West Yorkshire, the kind of home you would pass a thousand times without a second glance. And that is exactly what makes its story so frightening. Because inside this unremarkable house, an ordinary family was reportedly terrorized by what has been called one of the most violent poltergeists in European history: the entity known as the Black Monk.

The ordinariness is the horror. There is no brooding architecture to blame, no obvious reason. Just a normal family in a normal house, and something inside it that would not leave them alone.

The Haunting Begins

The disturbances are said to have begun in 1966, when the Pritchard family lived at 30 East Drive. It started small and strange: a fine chalk-like dust drifting down at waist height, pools of water appearing on the kitchen floor with no source, no leak, no explanation. Plumbers and family members were baffled. Then it escalated.

Objects began to move and fly. Furniture shifted on its own. Cupboards shook, lights switched themselves on and off, and sudden cold swept through rooms. Green foam reportedly oozed from taps and toilets. The activity grew bolder and more aggressive over time, building from eerie nuisance into something that felt genuinely malevolent, an unseen presence that seemed to want to be noticed, and feared.

The Black Monk

As the haunting continued, it acquired an identity. Local legend connected the entity to a monk said to have lived in the Pontefract area centuries earlier, a monk reputed to have been executed for a terrible crime against a young girl. Whether or not there is any truth to it, the family and investigators came to call the presence the Black Monk, and some reported glimpsing a tall, dark, robed figure in the house.

The most disturbing incidents centered on the Pritchards' teenage daughter, Diane. In the haunting's most infamous moment, Diane was allegedly dragged up the staircase by an unseen force, with witnesses present, reportedly leaving red marks on her neck. For a family simply trying to live in their home, it was a terrifying escalation, physical violence from something they could not see or fight.

In the most infamous moment, the teenage daughter was reportedly dragged up the stairs by an unseen force, in front of witnesses, leaving red marks on her throat. The Black Monk had stopped merely frightening them.

Fame and the Film

The events at 30 East Drive became one of Britain's most famous hauntings, documented in books and eventually dramatized in the 2012 film "When the Lights Went Out," inspired by the case. The story endures because it is so domestic and so relatable, there is no exotic setting to distance yourself from it. It happened to a normal family, in a normal house, doing normal things, which is precisely why it unsettles people so deeply.

What sets the Pontefract case apart from gentler hauntings is its sheer aggression. This was not a sad figure drifting down a hall. It was water, foam, flying crockery, shaking furniture, and a child hauled up a staircase, an entity that seemed to escalate deliberately, testing how far it could go.

The House Today

30 East Drive still stands, still ordinary from the outside. After the events made it famous, the house was purchased and is now available for paranormal investigators and curious visitors to rent and stay overnight, to spend the dark hours in the very rooms where the Black Monk is said to have raged. Many who have stayed report their own experiences, keeping the legend very much alive.

It remains a quiet house on a quiet street, surrounded by neighbors living their everyday lives. And that is the lasting chill of 30 East Drive: the reminder that the most frightening haunted place need not be a ruined castle on a hill. Sometimes it is a perfectly normal home, identical to a million others, where something invisible once decided to make a family's life a nightmare.
